## DESIGN CHECKLIST

Before you post:

- [ ] **Logo is crisp** at 1080px width (no pixilation)
- [ ] **Text is readable** on mobile (test on phone)
- [ ] **Booth number is clear** and prominent
- [ ] **Dates are correct** (Aug 22–23, 2026)
- [ ] **Color contrast is high** (dark text on light, light text on dark)
- [ ] **No typos** in copy
- [ ] **CTA is clear** (link in bio, come see us, sign up, etc.)
- [ ] **Brand logo is consistent** across all your posts
- [ ] **Font sizes match hierarchy** (headline > body > supporting text)
- [ ] **Image (if used) is high-quality** and on-brand

---

## FILE SPECIFICATIONS FOR UPLOAD

- **Format:** PNG or JPG (1080 × 1350px)
- **Color Space:** sRGB (for Instagram compatibility)
- **File Size:** Under 5MB
- **Quality:** 72–96 DPI is fine for social (no need for print quality)
- **Transparency:** Not needed (Instagram shows solid backgrounds)
