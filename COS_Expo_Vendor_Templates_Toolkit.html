How to Use These Templates

1. Choose Your Angle

Pick 1–3 templates that match your goals. Want booth traffic? Use 1, 2, or 6. Want signups? Use 3. Want to position yourself? Use 5 or 8.

2. Customize Copy

Swap in your booth number, discount, or service. Keep headlines punchy (under 8 words). Follow the customization guide in each template.

3. Add Your Logo & Brand

Logo goes where marked [YOUR LOGO]. Swap in your colours if desired. Keep it clean and readable on mobile.

4. Add Charity Message

Include in your caption: "100% of ticket proceeds benefit Pikes Peak Suicide Prevention." This is required in every Expo post.

5. Choose Your Platform

Use these in Canva, Photoshop, Adobe XD, or any design tool. Dimensions are 1080×1350px (Instagram portrait).

6. Post & Track

Post to Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn. Watch which templates get engagement. Repeat what works. All copy is yours to adapt.

Technical Specs

Dimensions & Setup

  • Carousels & Single Posts: 1080 × 1350px (Instagram portrait)
  • Stories: 1080 × 1920px (if you adapt for Stories)
  • Color Space: sRGB (for Instagram compatibility)
  • File Format: PNG or JPG (under 5MB)
  • DPI: 72–96 DPI is fine for social

Brand Colors (Use These)

  • Deep Purple: #55009B (primary background)
  • Electric Cyan: #00F2F8 (energy/accents)
  • Royal Blue: #0261C6 (secondary headlines)
  • Black: #000000 (text/contrast)
  • White: #FFFFFF (breathing room)

Typography

  • Headlines: Montserrat 800 (bold, all caps), 52px minimum
  • Body Text: Open Sans 400 (regular), 32px minimum
  • CTAs: Montserrat 700 (bold, all caps)

Design Rules

  • Minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for all text
  • No text in bottom 120px (Instagram UI overlap)
  • Minimum 60px padding on all sides (80px preferred)
  • Keep one idea per slide — don't overcrowd
  • Test text readability on mobile before posting
