Instructions

How to Use These Templates

Four steps. Start posting today — every week before August 22 builds more reach.

01
Click Your Template Set

Click "Open in Canva" above your section (Vendor or Sponsor). Sign in if prompted — Canva is free.

02
Duplicate & Customize

Choose your preferred design. Replace the logo placeholder with yours. Add your booth number where indicated. Update any copy to match your voice.

03
Download & Export

Download as PNG or JPG at 1080×1350px. Use Canva's built-in scheduler or export to your preferred platform.

04
Post & Tag the Expo

Tag @cosfitnessexpo in every post. Hashtags: #COSFitnessExpo #ColoradoSprings #FitnessEvent #GreatWolfLodge

Scheduling

Schedule & CRM Integration

Pick the tool that fits your workflow. The goal is simple: get posts scheduled before you leave this page.

Canva Scheduler
Best for: Quick direct scheduling, no extra tools
  • Design your post in Canva using the templates above
  • Click "Share" → "Schedule" directly inside Canva
  • Connect Instagram, Facebook, or LinkedIn
  • Set your date and time — Canva publishes automatically
  • Free with Canva account; Canva Pro unlocks bulk scheduling via CSV
GoHighLevel (GHL)
Best for: CRM-integrated campaigns + automation
  • Download posts as PNG from Canva
  • Upload to GHL Social Planner → New Post
  • Create a "COS Expo Promo" campaign folder for organization
  • Schedule across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn in one flow
  • Use GHL workflows to trigger email reminders alongside your post cadence
Meta Business Suite
Best for: Instagram + Facebook, zero extra cost
  • Go to business.facebook.com
  • Select "Create Post" → upload your downloaded PNG
  • Choose both Instagram and Facebook pages simultaneously
  • Set schedule date and time (up to 75 days out)
  • Completely free — no third-party tool required
